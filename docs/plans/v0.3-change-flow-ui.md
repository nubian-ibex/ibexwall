# v0.3: a change bar, a review panel and a steadier layout

Status: approved by the maintainer's go-ahead on the mockup
(`mockups/07-redesenho-mudancas.html`), 2026-11-19. Nothing here changes
what the daemon does; it is the app's interface only.

## Why

Feedback on 0.2.0: the buttons that apply a change (Simulate, Apply,
Confirm) are not intuitive and have no place of their own in the layout; the
version is not shown anywhere; screens look uneven.

What the screens did: each one grew its own inline review block at the end
of a long page (DNS and Hardening put it below everything), with three
buttons that appeared one after another in the same card. The maintainer
could not tell which step they were in or where the action would be.

## Design

1. **Version.** The sidebar footer shows "Version 0.2.x" next to the service
   status, from `GetInfo`. No new call.
2. **One place for changes.** A store, `lib/changes.svelte.ts`, holds the
   drafts a person has queued: `{ id, source, plugin, patch, count, label }`.
   Screens that gather several choices (Hardening, DNS) keep their draft in
   the store as the person ticks; screens with one deliberate action
   (Firewall switch, port, Apps block, Plugins toggle, Assistant, Detected)
   call `changes.propose(...)` and the review opens at once.
3. **Change bar.** Fixed at the bottom of the content area on every screen,
   shown only while drafts exist: "3 changes ready", Discard, and the primary
   "Review and apply". The sidebar item of a screen with drafts shows a count.
4. **Review panel.** A panel on the right with the three steps always
   visible: 1 Review, 2 Apply, 3 Keep. Review plans every draft (one plan per
   plugin, as `HardeningFlow` does today) and shows the diff and risks;
   "Apply n changes" is fixed in the panel footer. Applying runs the
   transactions in order. Keep shows the countdown from the core's deadline
   with large Keep and Undo buttons; an unanswered deadline is undone by the
   core and the panel says so. A failure part-way keeps an accurate list, as
   `HardeningFlow` does now, and a retry repeats only what is open.
5. **Layout.** Every screen uses one page header (title, summary, optional
   action on the right) and one width, 1100 px. Narrow overrides (760 px on
   DNS and Firewall) go.
6. **DNS in tabs.** Provider, Block lists, Browsers, Own servers. The draft
   is built from the whole form, so changes in any tab add to one bar.

## What stays

- The daemon, the API, polkit and the plan, apply, confirm, rollback
  sequence. The panel calls the same `planChange`, `apply`, `confirm` and
  `rollback`.
- The 60 second keep window and the rule that nothing changes before apply.
- Every string in the five languages; targets of 44 px; keyboard use, with
  focus moved into the panel when it opens and back to the bar when it
  closes; `Escape` closes it before anything is applied.

## Steps

1. **S1 Shell:** the version in the sidebar footer, the page header
   component, one width. Tests: shell test for the version.
2. **S2 Store, bar and panel:** the store, `ChangeBar`, `ReviewPanel`;
   `ChangeFlow` becomes a button that proposes; `HardeningFlow` is removed;
   Hardening and the single-action screens move over. Tests: store and panel
   unit tests (plan failure, partial apply, partial undo, deadline passing,
   double click), screen tests, Playwright.
3. **S3 DNS tabs:** the tabs and the draft from the whole form.
4. **S4 Verification:** `pnpm check`, lint, tests, build, Playwright; the
   `desktop` phase on the Fedora and Ubuntu VMs, with screenshots of the
   bar and the panel from a real install; docs and CHANGELOG.

## Risks

- Many tests click "Apply" inside screens; they move to the panel. The
  e2e flows are rewritten once, not patched per screen.
- A draft kept in the store survives a screen change on purpose (the bar
  stays), which also means stale drafts: a screen that reloads removes the
  drafts whose items were applied, and Discard clears the rest.
- The panel must not hide the password dialog flow (ADR-0045): the waiting
  notice stays visible inside the panel while a change waits.

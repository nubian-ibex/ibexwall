# v0.7: Secure DNS with NetworkManager and no systemd-resolved

Status: **proposed, not approved**. No code until the maintainer decides. It
assumes a global DNS drop-in of IbexWall's own in
`/etc/NetworkManager/conf.d`, and that dnsmasq, unmanaged and static setups
stay refused.

## Milestones

1. **M0 spike** (no product code): on an Arch guest with NetworkManager and
   without systemd-resolved, confirm that the drop-in reaches
   `/etc/resolv.conf` after a reload and is gone after removing it; check
   per-connection and router-advertised servers, a VPN and the connectivity
   check. Exit: a written note and the supported cases.
2. **M1 detect**: the new backend and its refusals. Verify: tree fixtures.
3. **M2 plan, verify, rollback, failsafe.** Verify: plugin tests and e2e.
4. **M3 surfaces**: the screen's texts in five locales, the guide.
5. **M4 VM phase**: `f3` on that guest, with a packet capture and the killed
   resolver.

## Acceptance

- No clear DNS leaves the machine with Secure DNS applied.
- A rollback, the failsafe and uninstall leave NetworkManager as it was.
- Machines in an unsupported variant are told why and nothing changes.
- `f3`, `f10` and `desktop` green on all supported distributions.

# v0.7: Secure DNS with NetworkManager and no systemd-resolved

Status: **approved** on 2026-11-26 (ADR-0051, option 1), in progress. A global
DNS drop-in of IbexWall's own in `/etc/NetworkManager/conf.d`; dnsmasq,
unmanaged and static setups stay refused. The M0 results are in the ADR; the
VM work is the `dns-nm` phase (Fedora and Arch).

## Verification so far (2026-11-27, branch v0.7.0-dev)

- The `dns-nm` VM phase (`cargo xtask vm-test --distro fedora|arch --phase
  dns-nm`) is green on both: 14 scenarios each, with the packages installed
  (SELinux enforcing on Fedora), the failsafe on both backends, no clear DNS
  on the wire, a refusal probe, the undo after a reboot, and removal.
- The spike ran on NetworkManager 1.58.1 (EndeavourOS and the Fedora guest),
  recorded in ADR-0051.
- The `f3` phase on Fedora (systemd-resolved) is green (17 scenarios): no
  regression in the resolved path.
- Known limits of the backend: search domains of the networks stop being
  used while Secure DNS is applied (said in the app and the guide); split
  DNS stays available through IbexWall's own routes. AppArmor (Ubuntu) has
  code-review coverage only for this path.

## Milestones

1. **M0 spike** (no product code): on an Arch guest with NetworkManager and
   without systemd-resolved, confirm that the drop-in reaches
   `/etc/resolv.conf` after a reload and is gone after removing it; check
   per-connection and router-advertised servers, a VPN and the connectivity
   check. Exit: a written note and the supported cases.
2. **M1 detect**: the new backend and its refusals. Verify: tree fixtures.
3. **M2 plan, verify, rollback, failsafe.** Verify: plugin tests and e2e.
4. **M3 surfaces**: the screen's texts in five locales, the guide.
5. **M4 VM phase**: `f3` on that guest, with a packet capture and the killed
   resolver.

## Acceptance

- No clear DNS leaves the machine with Secure DNS applied.
- A rollback, the failsafe and uninstall leave NetworkManager as it was.
- Machines in an unsupported variant are told why and nothing changes.
- `f3`, `f10` and `desktop` green on all supported distributions.
